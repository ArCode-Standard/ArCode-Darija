# Code of Conduct

The IDRIS project welcomes people interested in Moroccan Darija and responsible language technology.

Expected behavior:
- Respect regional, cultural, linguistic, and personal differences.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Communicate constructively and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, consent, licensing, and data ownership.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and publishing private or non-consensual information are unacceptable.

This policy applies to project spaces, issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related public interactions. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect contributors and the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the GitHub organization contact channel. Do not post sensitive details in public issues.
