## Image treatment

- Images are presented with dark overlays and restrained saturation to match the IDRIS visual system.
- Human images are selected for rear-view / non-identifying compositions; do not crop or retouch them to reveal faces.
- The photographs are not represented as original IDRIS photography or as images of project contributors.
