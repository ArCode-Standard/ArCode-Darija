# IDRIS Research Charter

**Status:** Initial public charter  
**Project:** IDRIS — Moroccan Darija Research Initiative  
**Stewardship:** ArCode Standard  
**Project lead:** Ismail Benali  
**Scope:** Moroccan Darija

## 1. Identity

IDRIS is an independent Moroccan research and engineering initiative led within ArCode Standard. It is not presented as a university, accredited research institution, registered company, or government programme.

The initiative welcomes community participation and future academic or technical collaboration. A conversation, email exchange, or informal contribution is not a formal partnership.

## 2. Purpose

IDRIS investigates how Moroccan Darija is spoken, written, represented in digital resources, and handled by language technologies. Its long-term technical ambition is to develop and evaluate tools and models that can understand and generate Darija responsibly.

## 3. Research principles

- **Speaker-centred:** respect speakers, regional variation, context, and changing usage.
- **Evidence-led:** distinguish implemented work from plans and hypotheses.
- **Rights-aware:** record provenance, license, consent, and permitted use for every data source.
- **Privacy-preserving:** collect the minimum information needed and explain processing before collection.
- **Reproducible:** document methods, versions, evaluation data, and limitations.
- **Open to scrutiny:** invite corrections and publish limitations alongside results.

## 4. Data and participant protection

Survey participation is not blanket consent to use responses for model training. Any future collection must have a published purpose, privacy notice, retention period, access policy, and appropriate legal review. Personal conversations, identifying details, and sensitive information should not be solicited in open-text responses.

Third-party resources must not be incorporated until their terms and rights have been reviewed. Public availability does not itself grant permission to reuse a dataset.

## 5. Claims and release gates

The project will not claim a trained public model, API availability, benchmark performance, institutional partnership, or completed research result without verifiable evidence.

A model release requires, at minimum, documented data provenance and permissions, reproducible training details, held-out evaluation, a model card, and a clear statement of limitations.

## 6. Governance and contact

The project is currently stewarded by ArCode Standard under the leadership of Ismail Benali. Governance, contribution, and contact procedures may evolve as the initiative grows; changes should be documented publicly.
